# OCS-0000 — Executive Observation Contract Standard
**Status:** Ratified by Genesis IV-B3  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Canonical namespace:** `core.observation`

## Purpose
An observation is one atomic, immutable, provenance-bearing statement about reality. Observation answers **What exists?** It does not infer, speculate, recommend, or authorize.

## Observational universe
- **Live Reality:** platforms, services, networks, sensors, APIs, resources, users, runtime events.
- **Recorded Reality:** books, papers, PDFs, files, source code, images, audio, video, transcripts, databases, archives.
- **Executive Reality:** missions, capabilities, cognition, execution, governance.
- **Human Reality:** attributed operator and expert reports.

Reading books and files may produce canonical observations when source location, acquisition path, excerpt, confidence, and provenance are preserved.

## Requirements
Every observation preserves deterministic identity, domain, type, kind, subject, predicate, value, rich provenance, `observed_at`, `recorded_at`, confidence, severity, priority, context, schema version, and correction lineage.

Observations are atomic and immutable. Correction creates a new observation with `supersedes_observation_id`. Severity describes impact; priority describes handling urgency. Both timestamps are timezone-aware UTC.

`core.observation` is the constitutional owner. Legacy producers cross this boundary only through explicit adapters. New Observation definitions elsewhere are prohibited.

Evidence may reference observations; situations may aggregate them; reasoning may interpret admitted evidence. None may rewrite the original observation.
