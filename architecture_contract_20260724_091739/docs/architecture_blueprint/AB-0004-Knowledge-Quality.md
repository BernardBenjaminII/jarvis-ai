# AB-0004: Knowledge Quality

## Status

Draft

## Purpose

JARVIS must distinguish high-value knowledge from noise.

## Quality Dimensions

- authority
- freshness
- completeness
- confidence
- consistency
- diversity
- extraction quality
- provenance
- usefulness

## Authority

Trusted sources carry more weight.

Examples:

```text

WHO        100
