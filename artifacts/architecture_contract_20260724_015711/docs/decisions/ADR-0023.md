# ADR-0023 — Executive Evidence and Trust Model

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Decision Type:** Foundational Architectural Doctrine
**Supersedes:** None
**Related:** ADR-0000 (Architectural North Star), WP-0001 (Executive Operating System), ADR-0022 (Observation Engine), Genesis VI-A6 Executive Persistence

---

# Executive Summary

JARVIS is designed to function as an **Executive Operating System**, not merely an AI assistant.

An Executive Operating System cannot rely on assumptions, transient observations, or opaque model outputs when making significant decisions. Every executive action must be supported by evidence that is independently verifiable, traceable, and reproducible.

This ADR establishes the **Executive Evidence and Trust Model**, which defines how trust is established throughout the system.

---

# Decision

The Executive shall never trust information solely because it exists.

Trust shall only be established through deterministic verification.

Every subsystem responsible for making executive decisions shall separate:

* Collection
* Verification
* Interpretation
* Decision
* Action

into distinct architectural responsibilities.

---

# Motivation

Conventional AI systems typically follow this pattern:

```text
Prompt
    ↓
Model
    ↓
Answer
```

This architecture provides no guarantee that an answer is:

* correct
* reproducible
* justified
* traceable
* complete

JARVIS instead follows an evidence-driven executive model:

```text
Evidence
        ↓
Observation
        ↓
Verification
        ↓
Inference
        ↓
Decision
        ↓
Action
```

The Executive therefore produces not only decisions, but also the evidence supporting those decisions.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## Principle 1 — Evidence Before Trust

> Nothing is trusted because it exists.
>
> Everything is trusted because it has been verified.

The existence of information is never sufficient justification for executive action.

Examples include:

* persistence checkpoints
* retrieved knowledge
* observations
* sensor data
* external APIs
* LLM responses
* user-provided information

Every source is subject to verification appropriate to its domain.

---

## Principle 2 — Observations Before Conclusions

Architectural components responsible for discovering facts shall never interpret them.

Observation components record only what is objectively present.

Interpretation is delegated to independent evaluators.

Example:

```text
Scanner
    ↓
Observation
```

Never:

```text
Scanner
    ↓
Diagnosis
```

This separation preserves deterministic behavior while improving auditability.

---

## Principle 3 — Immutable Evidence

Evidence is immutable.

If evidence changes, it becomes **new evidence**.

Previously certified evidence is never modified.

Examples include:

* checkpoint records
* integrity reports
* executive observations
* reasoning evidence
* audit logs

Immutability preserves provenance and enables reproducible investigation.

---

## Principle 4 — Verification Before Action

No executive subsystem shall execute actions based on unverified information.

Verification precedes:

* recovery
* replay
* mission execution
* tool invocation
* autonomous actions
* executive recommendations

---

## Principle 5 — Decisions Are Explainable

Every significant executive decision shall be explainable through a complete chain of evidence.

Minimum explainability chain:

```text
Evidence
    ↓
Observation
    ↓
Verification
    ↓
Inference
    ↓
Decision
```

If this chain cannot be reconstructed, the decision is not considered fully justified.

---

# Executive Trust Pipeline

Every executive subsystem should conform to the following lifecycle:

```text
Collect
        ↓
Verify
        ↓
Evaluate
        ↓
Decide
        ↓
Act
        ↓
Record
```

Each stage has exactly one responsibility.

---

# Architectural Separation

Subsystems should separate responsibilities as follows:

```text
Scanner
        ↓
Evaluator
        ↓
Report
        ↓
Consumer
```

### Scanner

Collects facts.

### Evaluator

Applies policy.

### Report

Produces immutable evidence.

### Consumer

Performs downstream actions.

No single component should perform all four responsibilities.

---

# Executive Trust Levels

Executive evidence may be assigned trust classifications.

Baseline trust levels:

```text
UNVERIFIED

↓

VERIFIED

↓

CERTIFIED

↓

AUTHORITATIVE
```

Future ADRs may introduce additional trust classifications.

---

# Evidence Provenance

Executive evidence should preserve provenance whenever practical.

Examples include:

* acquisition source
* creation timestamp
* originating subsystem
* schema version
* integrity fingerprint
* parent relationships
* verification history

Loss of provenance reduces trust.

# Consequences

Adopting this doctrine provides:

* deterministic executive behavior
* reproducible decisions
* stronger auditability
* improved explainability
* modular verification
* consistent trust evaluation
* long-term architectural stability

The additional engineering complexity is accepted because executive reliability takes precedence over implementation convenience.

---

# Relationship to ADR-0000

This ADR extends, but does not replace, the Architectural North Star.

The two permanent doctrines of the Executive become:

> **Knowledge is permanent. Intelligence is upgradable.**

and

> **Every executive action must be justified by verifiable evidence.**

The first defines how knowledge evolves.

The second defines how knowledge becomes trustworthy.

Together they establish the philosophical foundation of the JARVIS Executive Operating System.

---

# Implementation Guidance

Future Genesis phases should reference this ADR whenever introducing new executive capabilities.

Subsystems should extend this trust model rather than creating independent verification mechanisms.

Trust is therefore treated as a shared executive capability rather than a subsystem-specific implementation.

---

# Certification Statement

Compliance with this ADR requires that executive decisions be supported by independently verifiable evidence, maintain traceable provenance, preserve immutable audit records, and remain reproducible under deterministic verification.

This doctrine is intended to remain valid across future generations of the JARVIS Executive Operating System.
