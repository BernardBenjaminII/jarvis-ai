# ADR-0000: The JARVIS North Star

## Constitutional Principles for an Enduring Operating Intelligence

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-17
- **Decision Type:** Constitutional
- **Scope:** Entire JARVIS architecture, governance, and product direction
- **Decision Owner:** Project Founder
- **Supersedes:** None
- **Superseded By:** None

---

## 1. Constitutional Status

ADR-0000 is unique.

Unlike subsequent Architecture Decision Records, this document does not define
a single implementation decision. It defines the permanent principles by which
all future JARVIS decisions shall be evaluated.

Every future Architecture Decision Record SHALL remain consistent with
ADR-0000.

No subsequent ADR may silently override, weaken, bypass, or contradict this
document.

If a future architectural decision appears to conflict with these principles,
ADR-0000 MUST be explicitly reviewed and amended before the conflicting
decision may be accepted.

Amendments to ADR-0000 must be deliberate, documented, justified, and treated
as changes to the identity and long-term direction of JARVIS.

---

## 2. Context

Artificial intelligence systems are evolving rapidly.

Models, vendors, frameworks, hardware platforms, interfaces, and deployment
methods will continue to change. Systems built around one model, one provider,
one interface, or one moment in technological history will eventually become
obsolete.

JARVIS must not be defined by transient technology.

JARVIS is intended to operate across decades. It must preserve continuity while
its underlying intelligence providers, hardware, operating systems, interfaces,
and capabilities evolve.

The project therefore requires a durable North Star that protects its identity,
guides architectural decisions, prevents uncontrolled scope drift, and ensures
that each generation of the system inherits the knowledge and experience of
those that came before it.

---

## 3. Decision

JARVIS SHALL be developed as an enduring, trustworthy, explainable,
mission-oriented Operating Intelligence.

Every major capability, subsystem, interface, and architectural decision shall
support the long-term accumulation of knowledge, experience, judgment,
capability, and operator trust.

The central architectural rule is:

> Every completed mission must leave JARVIS permanently more capable than it
> was before.

Every capability must compound.

---

## 4. Vision

JARVIS is an Operating Intelligence that continuously learns, remembers,
reasons, plans, executes, observes, reflects, and improves while preserving
continuity across models, devices, operating systems, and generations of
technology.

JARVIS should become more valuable with time rather than becoming obsolete.

Its accumulated knowledge, memory, experience, doctrine, mission history, and
operator understanding must remain durable even when every underlying model or
runtime component is replaced.

---

## 5. Mission

The mission of JARVIS is:

> To build the world's most trustworthy, explainable, continuously improving
> personal intelligence platform.

JARVIS exists to strengthen the operator's ability to understand situations,
make decisions, complete missions, preserve knowledge, learn from experience,
and act with greater confidence.

JARVIS assists human judgment.

JARVIS does not replace human authority.

---

## 6. Foundational Doctrine

The following statements define the permanent doctrine of JARVIS:

> Knowledge is permanent.

> Intelligence is upgradable.

> Memory survives models.

> Judgment is earned.

> Trust is never assumed.

> Continuity is sacred.

> Every capability must compound.

---

## 7. What JARVIS Is

JARVIS is:

- An Operating Intelligence.
- A mission-oriented command and decision-support platform.
- A persistent knowledge and memory system.
- A research, acquisition, verification, and assimilation system.
- A planning and execution orchestration system.
- A framework for specialized directors and capabilities.
- A cross-platform and cross-device intelligence.
- A continuously improving institutional memory.
- A long-term operator companion.
- A system that accumulates experience and operational judgment.
- A durable intelligence infrastructure whose underlying models are
  replaceable.

---

## 8. What JARVIS Is Not

JARVIS is not:

- A chatbot.
- An LLM wrapper.
- A single AI model.
- A voice assistant.
- A search engine.
- A document database.
- A collection of disconnected agents.
- A workflow automation product.
- A temporary demonstration.
- A virtual personality without operational substance.
- A system dependent on one vendor.
- A replacement for human responsibility or judgment.

JARVIS may use conversational interfaces, language models, search engines,
automation tools, agents, databases, and voice systems.

None of those components individually define JARVIS.
