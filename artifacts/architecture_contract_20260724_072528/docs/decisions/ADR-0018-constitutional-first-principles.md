# ADR-0018 — Constitutional First Principles

## Status

Accepted

## Context

As the JARVIS Executive matured beyond a software project into a governed executive architecture, it became necessary to establish a permanent constitutional foundation independent of implementation technology.

## Decision

The project adopts FP-0000 as the highest-level engineering standard governing all future architectural decisions.

FP-0000 defines:

- Executive Identity
- Executive Oath
- Constitutional First Principles
- Engineering Consequences
- Revision Policy

All future standards derive from FP-0000.

## Consequences

Architectural discussions shall reference FP-0000.

Implementation shall follow architecture.

Architecture shall follow standards.

Standards shall follow governance.
