# Executive Summary

The Executive Constitutional Assessment Methodology establishes
the constitutional doctrine governing the evaluation,
measurement, verification, and analysis of Executive
Missions, Executive Decisions, Executive Planning,
Executive Resources, and Executive Execution throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Assessment determines whether constitutional execution has
actually achieved Executive intent.

Where Execution performs organizational activity, Assessment
determines organizational effectiveness.

Assessment therefore transforms completed action into
institutional understanding.

Accordingly, this methodology establishes the permanent
constitutional principles, architecture, lifecycle,
procedures, governance, historical preservation, and
certification governing Executive Assessment.
