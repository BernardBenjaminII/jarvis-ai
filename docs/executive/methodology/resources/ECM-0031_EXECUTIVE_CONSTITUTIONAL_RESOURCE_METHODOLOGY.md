# Executive Summary

The Executive Constitutional Resource Methodology
establishes the permanent constitutional doctrine
governing the identification, allocation,
prioritization, stewardship, optimization,
preservation, and continual improvement of Executive
Resources throughout the Executive Operating System.

Executive Resources transform coordinated Executive
Plans into executable organizational capability.

Where Executive Planning determines what resources are
required, Executive Resources determine what is
available, what must be acquired, how resources are
allocated, how competing demands are balanced, and how
organizational capability is sustained throughout
Mission execution.

Executive Resources therefore serve as the
constitutional capability responsible for ensuring
that every Mission possesses the personnel,
equipment, knowledge, finances, infrastructure,
information, and time necessary to achieve Mission
success while preserving constitutional stewardship
and organizational sustainability.

Accordingly, this methodology establishes the
permanent constitutional principles, architecture,
lifecycle, framework, procedures, historical
preservation, and certification governing Executive
Resources.
