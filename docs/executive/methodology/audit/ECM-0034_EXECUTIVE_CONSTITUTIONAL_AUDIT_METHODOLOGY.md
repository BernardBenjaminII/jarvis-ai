establishes the permanent constitutional doctrine
governing the independent verification, compliance
assessment, constitutional assurance, accountability,
integrity validation, preservation, and continual
improvement of Executive Audit throughout the
Executive Operating System.

constitutional assurance.

Where Executive Assessment determines organizational
performance, Executive Audit independently verifies
that every constitutional capability, methodology,
decision, plan, resource, execution activity, and
assessment complies with the Executive Constitution
and preserves institutional integrity.

Executive Audit therefore serves as the permanent
constitutional safeguard responsible for protecting
constitutional legitimacy, Executive accountability,
organizational trust, institutional continuity, and
continual constitutional improvement.

Accordingly, this methodology establishes the
permanent constitutional principles, architecture,
lifecycle, framework, procedures, historical
preservation, and certification governing Executive
Audit.
