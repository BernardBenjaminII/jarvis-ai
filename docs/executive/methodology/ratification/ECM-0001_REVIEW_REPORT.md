# Executive Review Declaration

This Executive Review Report records the formal constitutional review of the
Executive Constitutional Methodology (ECM-0001).

The purpose of this review is to determine whether ECM-0001 faithfully
establishes a complete, internally consistent, implementation-independent,
technology-independent methodology for constitutional engineering within the
Executive Operating System.

This review evaluates the methodology itself.

It does not evaluate any individual constitution derived from the methodology.

The outcome of this report shall determine whether ECM-0001 is suitable for
Executive Ratification.

The Executive Review Authority exists to ensure that constitutional authority
is established through disciplined evaluation rather than assumption.

artifact accurately represents institutional intent, satisfies constitutional
requirements, and is suitable for adoption as governing authority.

assurance mechanism of the Executive Operating System.

ECM-0001.

operates independently from drafting activities.

- evaluate constitutional correctness
