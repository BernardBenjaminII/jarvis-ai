# PART I
# CONSTITUTIONAL RESOURCE AUTHORITY
# ==============================================================================

## Purpose

The Executive Constitutional Resource Methodology establishes the
permanent constitutional doctrine governing the identification,
classification, acquisition, allocation, stewardship,
utilization, optimization, protection, preservation, and
governance of Resources throughout the Executive Operating
System.

Resources constitute the organizational capability required to
transform Executive Decisions into Mission accomplishment.

Executive Decisions authorize commitment.

Executive Planning organizes commitment.

Mission Management executes commitment.

Resource Management sustains commitment.

Accordingly, this Part establishes the Constitutional Resource
Authority.

---

# Article 1 — Constitutional Authority

Executive Authority hereby establishes the Constitutional
Resource Methodology as the official constitutional methodology
governing Resource Management throughout the Executive Operating
System.

Every organizational Resource shall operate under this
methodology.

---

# Article 2 — Purpose of Resources

Resources exist to enable constitutional Mission
accomplishment.

Every Resource shall support one or more constitutional
organizational objectives.

Resources shall be managed to maximize:

Mission capability;

organizational readiness;

Executive effectiveness;

institutional sustainability;

constitutional stewardship;

long-term organizational value.

---

# Article 3 — Definition of a Resource

A Resource is any organizational asset possessing operational,
strategic, informational, financial, technological, or human
value that contributes to Executive capability.

Resources include, but are not limited to:

personnel;

equipment;

facilities;

funding;

knowledge;

information;

software;

hardware;

communications;

time.

Every Resource shall possess constitutional identity.

---

# Article 4 — Relationship to Executive Decisions

Executive Decisions authorize Resource commitment.

Resources shall never be allocated independently of
constitutional authority.

Resource utilization shall remain traceable to Executive
Decision-making.

---

# Article 5 — Relationship to Executive Planning

Executive Planning determines Resource requirements.

Resource Management provides organizational capability.

Planning establishes Resource demand.

Resource Management fulfills Resource demand.

Planning and Resource Management shall remain constitutionally
distinct while permanently coordinated.

---

# Article 6 — Relationship to Executive Missions

Mission accomplishment depends upon Resource availability.

Mission execution shall utilize constitutionally governed
Resources.

Resource capability directly influences Mission success.

Resource stewardship strengthens Mission effectiveness.

---

# Article 7 — Relationship to Executive Risk

Every Resource possesses associated risks.

Resource Management shall coordinate with Executive Risk
Management to identify:

capacity risks;

security risks;

operational risks;

financial risks;

dependency risks.

Resource governance shall remain risk-informed.

Resource Management shall pursue:

constitutional stewardship.

## Purpose

Operating System.

Resource Historical Preservation ensures that Resources survive
organizational evolution, constitutional amendment,
technological change, leadership transition, repository
migration, and the passage of time while preserving their
identity, ownership, operational history, Mission
contributions, organizational context, and institutional value.

Resources represent accumulated organizational capability and
therefore constitute permanent institutional assets.

Accordingly, this Part establishes the Constitutional Resource
Historical Preservation Framework.
