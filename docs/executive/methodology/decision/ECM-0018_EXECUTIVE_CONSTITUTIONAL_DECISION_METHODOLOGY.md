# PART I
# CONSTITUTIONAL DECISION AUTHORITY
# ==============================================================================

## Purpose

The Constitutional Decision Authority establishes Executive
Decision Making as the permanent constitutional capability
responsible for transforming justified Executive Reasoning into
authorized Executive Actions throughout the Executive Operating
System.

Executive Decisions are the constitutional mechanism through which
organizational judgment becomes organizational action.

Executive Reasoning establishes justified recommendations.

Executive Decisions authorize commitment, allocate resources,
accept accountability, establish priorities, direct execution,
and define organizational intent.

Executive Decisions shall remain explainable, accountable,
traceable, evidence-supported, constitutionally governed, and
subject to continual review.

Accordingly, Executive Decision Making forms the constitutional
bridge between Executive Reasoning and Mission Execution.

This Part establishes the constitutional authority, purpose,
responsibilities, governance, and scope of Executive Decision
Making.

---

# Article 1 — Constitutional Authority

Executive Authority hereby establishes Executive Decision Making
as a permanent constitutional capability of the Executive
Operating System.

Executive Decision Making shall govern the authorization,
approval, prioritization, accountability, execution authority,
and constitutional governance of organizational decisions.

Executive Decision Making shall remain under Executive Authority.

---

# Article 2 — Purpose of Executive Decision Making

Executive Decision Making exists to transform justified Executive
Reasoning into authorized organizational action.

Executive Decision Making shall:

authorize action;

accept accountability;

allocate resources;

prioritize objectives;

resolve alternatives;

establish organizational direction;

approve execution;

support mission success;

strengthen governance;

improve institutional capability.

Decision transforms judgment into action.

---

# Article 3 — Definition of Executive Decision

Executive Decision is defined as:

> The constitutionally authorized commitment to a justified course
> of action based upon validated Executive Reasoning for the
> purpose of accomplishing Executive objectives while maintaining
> accountability, transparency, explainability, and constitutional
> governance throughout the Executive Operating System.

Executive Decisions authorize action.

Executive Actions execute decisions.

---

# Article 4 — Relationship to Executive Reasoning

Executive Decisions shall originate exclusively from validated
Executive Reasoning.

Reasoning establishes justification.

Executive Decisions authorize organizational commitment.

Reasoning precedes Decision.

# Article 5 — Relationship to Mission Execution

Decisions.

Executive Decisions provide:

mission authority;

resource authorization;

organizational priorities;

execution constraints;

success criteria;

Executive accountability.

constitute Executive Mission Execution.

---

# Article 6 — Relationship to Executive Planning

Executive Planning prepares organizational alternatives.

Executive Decisions authorize which plan shall become

Planning develops options.

Decision selects commitment.

---

# Article 7 — Relationship to Executive Risk
