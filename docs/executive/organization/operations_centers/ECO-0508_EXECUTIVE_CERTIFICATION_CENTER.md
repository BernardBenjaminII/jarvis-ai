# Executive Summary

The Executive Certification Center (ECC-C) is the
permanent constitutional operational center
responsible for coordinating the operational
certification of every capability throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Engineering produces capability.

Verification measures capability.

Certification authorizes capability.

The Executive Certification Center serves as the
operational headquarters where certification
activities are coordinated, evidence is assembled,
readiness is assessed, and certification packages
are prepared for review by the Executive
Certification Board.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Certification Center.

## Mission

The Executive Certification Center exists to
coordinate the operational certification of every
Executive capability.

Its Mission is to ensure that certification
decisions are supported by complete, objective,
traceable, and trustworthy evidence.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Center preserves:

certification integrity;

operational readiness;

verification transparency;

deployment confidence;

enterprise trust;

constitutional accountability.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Center shall:

coordinate certification activities;

assemble certification evidence;

monitor readiness;

track certification status;

prepare certification packages;

coordinate reviews;

maintain certification history;

strengthen enterprise confidence.
