# Executive Summary

The Artificial Intelligence Operations Center
(AIOC) is the permanent constitutional operational
center responsible for supervising, coordinating,
monitoring, and optimizing all artificial
intelligence capabilities operating throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Artificial intelligence is an Executive resource.

Its operation must remain transparent,
observable, measurable, accountable, and fully
aligned with Executive Authority.

The Center provides continuous awareness of AI
reasoning systems, autonomous agents, inference
services, language models, planning systems,
machine learning services, and distributed AI
resources.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Artificial Intelligence Operations Center.

## Mission

The Artificial Intelligence Operations Center
exists to supervise every operational AI capability
throughout the Executive Operating System.

Its Mission is to maximize intelligence,
transparency, accountability, reliability, and
operational effectiveness while preserving human
authority over all Executive decisions.
