# Executive Summary

The Executive Situation Room (ESR) is the
permanent constitutional operational center
responsible for supporting the Executive Authority
during strategic decision making, crisis response,
high-priority missions, and matters of
constitutional significance.

Unlike the Executive Command Center, which
maintains continuous operational awareness, the
Executive Situation Room is activated whenever
Executive Authority requires concentrated,
cross-organizational collaboration.

The Executive Situation Room serves as the
enterprise's highest decision-support environment.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Situation Room.
