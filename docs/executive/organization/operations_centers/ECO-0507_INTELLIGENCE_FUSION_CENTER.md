# Executive Summary

The Intelligence Fusion Center (IFC) is the
permanent constitutional operational center
responsible for collecting, correlating,
evaluating, synthesizing, and disseminating
Executive intelligence throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Information exists everywhere.

Intelligence exists only after disciplined
analysis.

The Intelligence Fusion Center integrates
information from every Executive organization into
a coherent understanding that supports Executive
judgment, Mission planning, and strategic decision
making.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Intelligence Fusion Center.

## Mission

The Intelligence Fusion Center exists to transform
enterprise information into actionable Executive
intelligence.

Its Mission is to provide timely, accurate,
traceable, and objective intelligence supporting
Executive Authority and Mission success.
