# Executive Summary

The Robotics Operations Center (ROC) is the
permanent constitutional operational center
responsible for supervising, coordinating,
monitoring, deploying, recovering, and sustaining
every robotic platform operating throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Robotic platforms extend Executive capability into
the physical world.

The Robotics Operations Center provides continuous
operational awareness of every robotic asset,
ensuring that physical capabilities remain
observable, coordinated, mission-ready, and fully
accountable to Executive Authority.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Robotics Operations Center.

## Mission

The Robotics Operations Center exists to direct,
coordinate, and sustain robotic operations
throughout the Executive Operating System.

Its Mission is to maximize robotic effectiveness
while preserving Mission safety, human oversight,
and operational accountability.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

robotic readiness;

Mission effectiveness;

fleet coordination;

human oversight;

operational reliability;

constitutional accountability.

# Primary Objectives

The Center shall:

coordinate robotic Missions;

manage robotic resources;

maintain operational readiness;

supervise autonomous operations;

coordinate maintenance;
