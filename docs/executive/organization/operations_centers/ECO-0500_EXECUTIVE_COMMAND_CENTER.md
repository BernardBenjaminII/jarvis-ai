# Executive Summary

The Executive Command Center (ECC) is the supreme
operational facility of the Executive Operating
System.

While Executive Offices provide leadership and
Executive Boards provide governance, the Executive
Command Center provides operational command and
enterprise situational awareness.

It is the single location where Executive Authority
maintains continuous awareness of the state of the
enterprise, directs Executive Missions, supervises
constitutional organizations, and coordinates
responses to strategic opportunities and crises.

The Executive Command Center serves as the primary
human interface to the Executive Operating System.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Command Center.

The Executive Command Center exists to maintain
continuous command, coordination, and awareness of

the information, tools, and decision support

organization.
