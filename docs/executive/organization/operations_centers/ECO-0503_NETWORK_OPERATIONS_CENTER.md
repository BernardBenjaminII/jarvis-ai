# Executive Summary

The Network Operations Center (NOC) is the
permanent constitutional operational center
responsible for maintaining the health,
availability, performance, and reliability of the
Executive Operating System infrastructure.

The Network Operations Center maintains continuous
situational awareness of enterprise computing,
storage, networking, communications, cloud
services, edge infrastructure, and distributed
Executive nodes.

Its purpose is to ensure uninterrupted enterprise
operations through proactive monitoring, rapid
response, operational coordination, and continuous
optimization.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Network Operations Center.

## Mission

The Network Operations Center exists to maintain
continuous availability of the Executive Operating
System.

Its Mission is to observe, coordinate, optimize,
and sustain enterprise infrastructure while
supporting uninterrupted Executive operations.

# Primary Objectives
