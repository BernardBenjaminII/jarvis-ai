# Executive Summary

The Department of Artificial Intelligence
Engineering is the permanent constitutional
organization responsible for designing,
implementing, integrating, evaluating, and
maintaining all artificial intelligence systems
throughout the Executive Operating System.

Artificial Intelligence expands Executive
capability.

Engineering transforms intelligence into reliable,
constitutional capability.

The Department develops AI systems that remain
transparent, explainable, secure, measurable, and
fully accountable to Executive Authority.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Artificial Intelligence
Engineering.

## Mission

The Department exists to engineer trustworthy
artificial intelligence systems that strengthen
every Executive capability.

Its Mission is to develop AI that operates safely,
constitutionally, transparently, and in service of
human decision-making.

human oversight;

# Primary Objectives

The Department shall:
