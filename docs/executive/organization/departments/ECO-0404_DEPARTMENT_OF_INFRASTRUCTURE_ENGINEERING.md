# Executive Summary

The Department of Infrastructure Engineering is
the permanent constitutional organization
responsible for engineering, operating, securing,
and sustaining the computational infrastructure of
the Executive Operating System.

Infrastructure is the foundation upon which every
Executive capability depends.

The Department provides computing platforms,
storage, networking, virtualization, cloud
services, edge systems, power management,
observability, and operational resilience required
to support enterprise execution.

Its purpose is to deliver a secure, reliable,
scalable, and continuously available infrastructure
capable of supporting every constitutional
organization.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Infrastructure Engineering.
