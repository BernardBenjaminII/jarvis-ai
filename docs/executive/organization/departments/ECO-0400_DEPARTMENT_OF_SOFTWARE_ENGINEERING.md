# Executive Summary

The Department of Software Engineering is the
permanent constitutional organization responsible
for designing, developing, verifying, maintaining,
and evolving software throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Software is the executable expression of
constitutional intent.

Every Executive capability ultimately becomes
software.

The Department transforms constitutional policy,
Executive decisions, operational requirements, and
architectural designs into reliable, secure,
maintainable software systems.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Software Engineering.

## Mission

The Department exists to engineer software that
implements the constitutional objectives of the
Executive Operating System.

Its Mission is to produce secure, deterministic,
maintainable, testable, and scalable software that
supports every Executive organization.
