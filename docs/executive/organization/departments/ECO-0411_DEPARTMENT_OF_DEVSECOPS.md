# Executive Summary

The Department of DevSecOps is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for
automating the secure development, integration,
delivery, deployment, operation, and continuous
improvement of every software capability within the
Executive Operating System.

Development creates capability.

Operations sustain capability.

Automation accelerates capability.

Security preserves capability.

The Department integrates engineering, security,
quality, infrastructure, and operations into one
continuous, observable, repeatable delivery
pipeline.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of DevSecOps.

## Mission

The Department exists to automate the secure
delivery and operation of Executive capabilities.

Its Mission is to continuously integrate, verify,
secure, deploy, observe, and improve enterprise
software while minimizing operational risk.

# Primary Objectives

The Department shall:
