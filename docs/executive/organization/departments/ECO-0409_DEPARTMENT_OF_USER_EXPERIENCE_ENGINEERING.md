# Executive Summary

The Department of User Experience Engineering is
the permanent constitutional organization
responsible for engineering every human interface
within the Executive Operating System.

Technology achieves its purpose only when humans
can understand, trust, and effectively employ it.

The Department designs, develops, evaluates, and
continuously improves interfaces that maximize
situational awareness, operational efficiency,
accessibility, usability, and decision support.

Its purpose is to ensure that every interaction
between human operators and the Executive Operating
System is intuitive, efficient, transparent, and
constitutionally aligned.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of User Experience Engineering.
