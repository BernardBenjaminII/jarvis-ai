# Executive Summary

The Department of Robotics Engineering is the
permanent constitutional organization responsible
for engineering autonomous and remotely operated
robotic systems throughout the Executive Operating
System.

Robotics extends Executive capability beyond the
digital domain.

The Department designs, develops, integrates,
certifies, and sustains robotic platforms capable
of operating safely, reliably, and constitutionally
within authorized environments.

Its purpose is to transform Executive intelligence
into physical capability while preserving safety,
human oversight, mission accountability, and
constitutional governance.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Robotics Engineering.

## Mission

The Department exists to engineer robotic systems
that extend the operational reach of the Executive
Operating System.

Its Mission is to develop robotic capabilities that
operate safely, intelligently, reliably, and in
support of Executive Missions.

The Department shall:
