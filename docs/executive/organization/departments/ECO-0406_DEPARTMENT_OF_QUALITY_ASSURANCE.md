# Executive Summary

The Department of Quality Assurance is the
permanent constitutional organization responsible
for assuring the quality, reliability, consistency,
and operational excellence of every capability
within the Executive Operating System.

Quality cannot be inspected into a system.

Quality must be engineered, measured, verified,
and continually improved.

The Department establishes quality processes,
coordinates enterprise quality activities,
identifies systemic deficiencies, and ensures that
every constitutional organization continuously
improves its performance.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Quality Assurance.

## Mission

The Department exists to ensure that every
Executive capability consistently satisfies
constitutional quality requirements.

Its Mission is to establish, monitor, evaluate,
and continually improve enterprise quality
throughout the Executive Operating System.
