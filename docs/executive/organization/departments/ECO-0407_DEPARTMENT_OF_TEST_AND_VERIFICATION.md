# Executive Summary

The Department of Test and Verification is the
permanent constitutional organization responsible
for objectively verifying that every capability of
the Executive Operating System satisfies its
approved requirements before certification.

Engineering produces capability.

Testing discovers defects.

Verification establishes confidence.

The Department conducts independent testing,
verification, validation, regression analysis,
performance evaluation, interoperability testing,
and readiness assessments while maintaining
objective technical independence from development
organizations.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Test and Verification.

## Mission

The Department exists to independently verify that
every Executive capability performs as intended.

Its Mission is to produce objective evidence of
functional correctness, reliability, safety,
performance, interoperability, and operational
readiness.
