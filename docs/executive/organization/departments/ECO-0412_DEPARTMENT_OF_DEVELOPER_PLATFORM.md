# Executive Summary

The Department of Developer Platform is the
permanent constitutional organization responsible
for engineering the tools, environments, services,
frameworks, and developer experience that enable
every engineering organization within the Executive
Operating System.

Developers create capability.

Platforms multiply developers.

The Department builds the engineering ecosystem
that allows software, AI, robotics, infrastructure,
knowledge, and integration teams to work rapidly,
consistently, and securely.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Developer Platform.
