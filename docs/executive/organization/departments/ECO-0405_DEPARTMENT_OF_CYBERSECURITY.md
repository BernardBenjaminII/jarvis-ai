# Executive Summary

The Department of Cybersecurity is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for
protecting the Executive Operating System against
digital threats while preserving the
confidentiality, integrity, availability, and
authenticity of enterprise information and
technology.

Security preserves capability.

Capability preserves Mission.

The Department designs, implements, operates,
monitors, and continually improves the defensive
cybersecurity posture of every constitutional
organization while ensuring that security remains
compatible with operational effectiveness.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Cybersecurity.

## Mission

The Department exists to defend the Executive
Operating System against cyber threats.

Its Mission is to engineer, operate, and continually
improve enterprise cybersecurity while enabling
Mission accomplishment through secure technology.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Department preserves:

enterprise security;

Mission assurance;

digital trust;

information protection;

operational resilience;

constitutional continuity.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Department shall:

protect enterprise systems;

reduce cyber risk;

monitor enterprise security;

respond to cyber incidents;

maintain defensive capabilities;

improve cyber resilience;

support Executive organizations;

strengthen constitutional security.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every capability creates exposure.

Every exposure requires defense.

Defense preserves civilization.
