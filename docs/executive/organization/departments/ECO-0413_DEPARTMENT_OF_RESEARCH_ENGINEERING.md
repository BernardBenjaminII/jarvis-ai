# Executive Summary

The Department of Research Engineering is the
permanent constitutional organization responsible
for transforming scientific research into
engineering capability throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Research discovers possibility.

Engineering transforms possibility into reality.

The Department bridges scientific investigation
and operational implementation through disciplined
experimentation, prototype development, technology
evaluation, and capability transition.

Its purpose is to ensure that new knowledge,
methods, and technologies mature into deployable
Executive capabilities while preserving scientific
rigor and engineering discipline.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Department of Research Engineering.

## Mission

The Department exists to engineer experimental
capabilities that advance the Executive Operating
System.

Its Mission is to evaluate emerging technologies,
develop prototypes, validate feasibility, and
transition successful research into operational
engineering organizations.

technology maturation;

institutional learning;

experimental integrity;

future capability.

# Primary Objectives

The Department shall:

evaluate emerging technology;

conduct engineering experiments;

develop proof-of-concept systems;
