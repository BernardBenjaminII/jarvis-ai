# Executive Summary

The Executive Architecture Council is the permanent
constitutional forum responsible for coordinating
enterprise architecture throughout the Executive
Operating System.

The Executive Architecture Board governs
architecture.

The Executive Architecture Council coordinates the
architects responsible for implementing that
governance.

Its purpose is to synchronize enterprise
architecture, technical design, organizational
structure, information architecture, interfaces,
standards, and long-term architectural evolution.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Architecture Council.

## Mission

The Executive Architecture Council exists to
coordinate enterprise architectural development.

Its Mission is to ensure that every architectural
decision contributes to one coherent Executive
Operating System.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Council preserves:

architectural consistency;

enterprise coherence;

technical integration;

organizational scalability;

interface stability;

constitutional structure.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Council shall:

coordinate enterprise architects;

review architectural initiatives;

resolve architectural conflicts;

maintain architectural standards;

recommend architectural improvements;

coordinate enterprise evolution;

advise Executive leadership;

strengthen constitutional architecture.

# Constitutional Law

Structure enables capability.

Coordinated architecture preserves civilization.
