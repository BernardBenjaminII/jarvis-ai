# Executive Summary

The Executive Constitutional Council is the
permanent constitutional forum responsible for
coordinating constitutional interpretation,
constitutional implementation, constitutional
maintenance, and constitutional evolution
throughout the Executive Operating System.

The Executive Constitutional Review Board governs
the Constitution.

The Executive Constitutional Council coordinates
the organizations responsible for implementing that
Constitution.

Its purpose is to synchronize constitutional
interpretation, doctrine implementation,
constitutional maintenance, constitutional
education, amendment preparation, governance
coordination, and institutional continuity across
the Executive Operating System.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Constitutional Council.
