# Executive Summary

The Executive Operations Council is the permanent
constitutional forum responsible for coordinating
the operational execution of the Executive
Operating System.

The Executive Leadership Council governs the
enterprise.

The Executive Operations Council governs the daily
conduct of Executive operations.

Its purpose is to synchronize Mission execution,
coordinate operational organizations, resolve
execution conflicts, allocate operational
priorities, and ensure that Executive Missions are
conducted as one unified enterprise.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Operations Council.

## Mission

The Executive Operations Council exists to
coordinate enterprise operations.

Its Mission is to synchronize operational
activities, maintain Mission readiness, resolve
execution conflicts, optimize operational
performance, and advise the Office of the Executive
Director regarding enterprise execution.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Council preserves:

operational unity;

Mission coordination;

execution readiness;

organizational synchronization;

enterprise responsiveness;

operational effectiveness.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Council shall:

coordinate operational activities;

review Mission execution;

prioritize operational workload;

resolve execution conflicts;

synchronize Directorate operations;

recommend operational improvements;

maintain Mission readiness;

strengthen enterprise execution.

# PART IV
# COUNCIL AUTHORITY
# ==============================================================================

The Council possesses constitutional authority to:

coordinate Mission execution;

recommend operational priorities;

synchronize Directorate activities;

facilitate operational cooperation;

resolve execution conflicts.

The Council shall not independently issue
Executive Orders nor alter constitutional policy.

---

# Decision Process

Operational recommendations should be achieved
through collaborative deliberation whenever
possible.

When consensus cannot be reached, unresolved issues
shall be documented and elevated to the Office of
the Executive Director.
