# Executive Summary

The Executive Certification Council is the
permanent constitutional forum responsible for
coordinating enterprise certification throughout
the Executive Operating System.

The Executive Certification Board governs
certification.

The Executive Certification Council coordinates the
organizations responsible for verification,
validation, testing, readiness assessment,
compliance review, release preparation, and
deployment certification.

Its purpose is to ensure that every capability
presented for constitutional certification is
complete, verified, traceable, and operationally
ready before submission to the Executive
Certification Board.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Certification Council.
