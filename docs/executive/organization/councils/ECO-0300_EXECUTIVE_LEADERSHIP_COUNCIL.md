# Executive Summary

The Executive Leadership Council is the permanent
constitutional forum responsible for coordinating
the senior leadership of the Executive Operating
System.

Unlike Executive Boards, which govern enterprise
policy, or Executive Directorates, which perform
operations, the Executive Leadership Council
synchronizes the activities of every constitutional
organization.

Its purpose is to ensure that Executive leadership
operates as a unified government rather than a
collection of independent organizations.

The Council resolves cross-organizational issues,
coordinates enterprise priorities, recommends
strategic action, and maintains unity of effort
throughout the Executive Operating System.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Leadership Council.

Executive leadership.

# COUNCIL AUTHORITY
# ==============================================================================

The Council possesses constitutional authority to:

coordinate Executive organizations;

recommend enterprise priorities;

facilitate leadership consensus;

resolve coordination conflicts;

synchronize constitutional activities.

The Council shall not independently issue
Executive Orders or modify constitutional policy.

---

# Decision Process

Council recommendations should, whenever practical,
be reached by informed consensus.

Where consensus cannot be achieved, alternative
positions shall be documented and submitted to the
Office of Executive Authority for final
determination.
