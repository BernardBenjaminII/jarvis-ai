# Executive Summary

The Executive Research Council is the permanent
constitutional forum responsible for coordinating
enterprise research throughout the Executive
Operating System.

The Executive Research Board governs research.

The Executive Research Council coordinates the
organizations responsible for conducting that
research.

Its purpose is to synchronize scientific inquiry,
technology investigation, experimentation,
knowledge discovery, acquisition campaigns,
prototype evaluation, validation activities, and
research collaboration across the Executive
Operating System.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Research Council.

## Mission

The Executive Research Council exists to coordinate
enterprise research.

Its Mission is to synchronize scientific
investigations, prioritize research execution,
coordinate experimentation, integrate discoveries,
and strengthen the research capability of the
Executive Operating System.

# Primary Objectives

The Council shall:

# ==============================================================================
