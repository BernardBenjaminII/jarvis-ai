# Executive Summary

The Executive Innovation Council is the permanent
constitutional forum responsible for coordinating
enterprise innovation throughout the Executive
Operating System.

The Executive Innovation Board governs innovation.

The Executive Innovation Council coordinates the
organizations responsible for transforming ideas
into operational capability.

Its purpose is to synchronize innovation programs,
technology adoption, prototype development,
organizational improvement, experimentation,
capability transition, and enterprise
transformation while maintaining constitutional
discipline and strategic alignment.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Innovation Council.

## Mission

The Executive Innovation Council exists to
coordinate enterprise innovation.

Its Mission is to synchronize innovation programs,
accelerate capability development, coordinate
organizational transformation, and strengthen the
competitive advantage of the Executive Operating
System.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Council preserves:

innovation coordination;

enterprise adaptability;

creative excellence;

capability evolution;

organizational agility;

strategic transformation.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Council shall:

coordinate innovation activities;

review innovation initiatives;

integrate emerging technologies;

resolve innovation conflicts;

recommend enterprise improvements;

coordinate capability transition;

advise Executive leadership;

strengthen organizational innovation.

# Constitutional Law

Coordination accelerates transformation.
