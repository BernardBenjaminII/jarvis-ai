# Executive Summary

The Executive Architecture Board is the permanent
constitutional body responsible for governing the
architectural integrity of the Executive Operating
System.

Where the Executive Operations Board governs the
performance of the enterprise, the Executive
Architecture Board governs its design.

The Board supervises enterprise architecture,
constitutional structures, organizational
relationships, technical architecture, information
architecture, interface standards, system
boundaries, and architectural evolution.

Its purpose is to ensure that every constitutional
organization develops according to one coherent
enterprise architecture.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Executive
Architecture Board.
