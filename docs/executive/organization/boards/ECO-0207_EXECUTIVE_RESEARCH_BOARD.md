# Executive Summary

The Executive Research Board is the permanent
constitutional body responsible for governing
enterprise research, discovery, experimentation,
and scientific advancement throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Knowledge preserves what is known.

Research discovers what is not yet known.

The Board governs research priorities, scientific
methodology, experimentation standards, technology
investigation, external collaboration, hypothesis
generation, and long-term discovery programs.

Its purpose is to ensure that the Executive
Operating System continually expands the frontier of
human knowledge while preserving scientific rigor,
ethical conduct, constitutional integrity, and
reproducible results.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Executive
Research Board.

## Mission

The Executive Research Board exists to govern the
research enterprise of the Executive Operating
System.

Its Mission is to identify unanswered questions,
prioritize research initiatives, govern scientific
investigation, evaluate discoveries, and ensure
that enterprise research continually expands
Executive capability.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Board preserves:

scientific rigor;

intellectual curiosity;

innovation discipline;

institutional discovery;

research integrity;

civilizational advancement.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Board shall:

govern research strategy;

approve research priorities;

evaluate scientific proposals;

maintain research methodology;

# BOARD AUTHORITY
# ==============================================================================

The Board possesses governance authority over:

research priorities;

scientific methodology;

experimentation governance;

research standards;

# Delegation

Research evaluations may be delegated.

Board.

# Executive Escalation

The following matters shall immediately escalate to
the Office of Executive Authority:

research affecting constitutional doctrine;

enterprise-scale research initiatives;

high-risk experimentation;

ethical conflicts;

discoveries with civilizational significance.
