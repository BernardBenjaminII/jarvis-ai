# Executive Summary

The Executive Ethics Board is the permanent
constitutional body responsible for governing the
ethical conduct of the Executive Operating System.

Capability without ethics creates danger.

Authority without ethics creates tyranny.

The Board governs ethical principles, responsible
conduct, constitutional values, human welfare,
Mission legitimacy, responsible innovation,
organizational integrity, and ethical review of
Executive activities.

Its purpose is to ensure that every Executive action
remains consistent with constitutional values,
human flourishing, lawful conduct, and responsible
exercise of Executive capability.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Executive
Ethics Board.

## Mission

The Executive Ethics Board exists to preserve the
ethical legitimacy of the Executive Operating
System.

Its Mission is to govern ethical standards, review
Executive activities for ethical consistency,
promote responsible innovation, protect human
interests, and ensure that constitutional authority
is exercised responsibly.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Board preserves:

ethical integrity;

human dignity;

organizational legitimacy;

responsible innovation;

constitutional values;

public trust.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Board shall:

govern ethical standards;

review ethical implications;

approve ethical guidance;

evaluate emerging ethical issues;

promote responsible conduct;

review constitutional values;

support Executive Authority;

continually strengthen ethical governance.

---

# Constitutional Law

Capability permits action.

Law permits authority.

Ethics determines whether action ought to occur.
