# Executive Summary

The Executive Certification Board is the permanent
constitutional body responsible for certifying the
readiness, compliance, quality, and constitutional
fitness of every enterprise capability prior to its
authorization for operational use.

Development creates capability.

Assessment measures capability.

Certification authorizes capability.

The Board serves as the constitutional gatekeeper
between creation and deployment.

No constitutional organization, software release,
AI model, robotic platform, knowledge corpus,
security policy, research result, operational
procedure, or Executive capability may enter
production without constitutional certification.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Executive
Certification Board.

## Mission

The Executive Certification Board exists to ensure
that every enterprise capability satisfies all
constitutional requirements before becoming
operational.

Its Mission is to independently verify readiness,
quality, safety, compliance, interoperability, and
constitutional alignment before authorizing
deployment.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Board preserves:

enterprise quality;

constitutional compliance;

operational readiness;

deployment confidence;

institutional trust;

Executive reliability.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Board shall:

govern certification standards;

approve certification criteria;

review readiness evidence;

authorize deployments;

maintain certification records;

monitor certification lifecycle;

support Executive Authority;

continually strengthen enterprise quality.

---

# Constitutional Law

Capability must be verified.

Verification enables certification.

Certification protects civilization.

# ==============================================================================

Board.

The following matters shall immediately escalate to
the Office of Executive Authority:

capability;

attempted deployment without certification;

systemic certification failure;

critical readiness deficiencies;

constitutional safety concerns;

Executive emergency authorization requests.
