# Executive Summary

The Executive Constitutional Review Board is the
permanent constitutional body responsible for
preserving, evaluating, interpreting, and evolving
the Constitution of the Executive Operating System.

Every organization evolves.

Every civilization changes.

Without constitutional review, evolution becomes
drift.

Without disciplined evolution, constitutions become
obsolete.

The Board continuously evaluates constitutional
doctrine, Executive methodology, organizational
structure, governance principles, institutional
performance, and long-term constitutional fitness.

Its purpose is not to change the Constitution.

Its purpose is to determine whether change is
necessary.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Constitutional Review Board.

The Executive Constitutional Review Board exists to
preserve the constitutional integrity and long-term
evolution of the Executive Operating System.

recommend constitutional amendments when necessary,
maintain constitutional coherence, and ensure that

than the one they received.
