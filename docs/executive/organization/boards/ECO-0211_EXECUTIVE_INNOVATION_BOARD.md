# Executive Summary

The Executive Innovation Board is the permanent
constitutional body responsible for governing the
generation, evaluation, maturation, and adoption of
innovative capabilities throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Research discovers.

Innovation applies.

Technology enables.

The Board governs the transition of ideas into
enterprise capability by evaluating novel concepts,
experimental technologies, organizational
improvements, operational methodologies, and
emerging opportunities.

Its purpose is to ensure that innovation remains
disciplined, strategically aligned, economically
justified, ethically responsible, and
constitutionally governed.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
permanent constitutional specification governing
the Executive Innovation Board.

## Mission

The Executive Innovation Board exists to govern the
enterprise innovation ecosystem.

Its Mission is to identify transformational
opportunities, mature promising innovations, guide
their constitutional adoption, and strengthen the
long-term competitive advantage of the Executive
Operating System.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Board preserves:

innovation discipline;

creative excellence;

enterprise adaptability;

competitive advantage;

organizational evolution;

constitutional alignment.

# Primary Objectives

The Board shall:

govern enterprise innovation;

review innovation proposals;

prioritize innovation initiatives;

evaluate organizational impact;

approve innovation portfolios;

monitor innovation maturity;

support strategic transformation;

continually improve innovation capability.

# ==============================================================================

innovation governance;

# Delegation

Innovation evaluations may be delegated.

Innovation governance accountability remains with
the Board.

# Executive Escalation

The following matters shall immediately escalate to
the Office of Executive Authority:

enterprise-transforming innovation;

innovation requiring constitutional amendment;

high-risk disruptive technology;

major organizational transformation;

innovation threatening enterprise stability;

civilizational innovation opportunities.
