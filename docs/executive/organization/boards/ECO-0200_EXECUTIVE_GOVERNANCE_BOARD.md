# Executive Summary

The Executive Governance Board is the permanent
constitutional body responsible for governing the
Executive Operating System at the enterprise level.

Unlike Executive Offices, which exercise executive
leadership, and Directorates, which perform
operational work, the Executive Governance Board
provides enterprise oversight, constitutional
alignment, strategic governance, and institutional
coordination.

The Board exists to ensure that every constitutional
organization remains aligned with Executive purpose,
constitutional doctrine, and long-term Mission
success.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Executive
Governance Board.
