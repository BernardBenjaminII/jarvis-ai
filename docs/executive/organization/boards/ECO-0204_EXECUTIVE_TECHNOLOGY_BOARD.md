# Executive Summary

The Executive Technology Board is the permanent
constitutional body responsible for governing the
technology ecosystem of the Executive Operating
System.

Technology is the physical and digital capability
through which constitutional intent becomes
operational reality.

The Board governs technology strategy, platform
selection, technology standards, research,
innovation, modernization, lifecycle management,
and long-term technological sustainability.

Its purpose is to ensure that every technology
adopted by the Executive Operating System advances
constitutional objectives while preserving
reliability, interoperability, security, and future
adaptability.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Executive
Technology Board.

## Mission

The Executive Technology Board exists to govern the
technology employed throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Its Mission is to ensure that technology decisions
remain constitutionally aligned, operationally
effective, economically sustainable, and capable of
supporting future generations of the Executive
Operating System.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Board preserves:

technological excellence;

platform stability;

enterprise interoperability;

innovation discipline;

technology sustainability;

constitutional alignment.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Board shall:

govern technology strategy;

approve technology standards;

evaluate emerging technologies;

oversee modernization;

maintain platform governance;

coordinate technology evolution;

manage technology risk;

support Executive capability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Technology enables capability.

Capability enables execution.

Disciplined technology enables enduring Executive
advantage.

# Delegation

Technology evaluations may be delegated.

the Board.

---

# Executive Escalation

The following matters shall immediately escalate to
the Office of Executive Authority:

enterprise technology failure;

critical platform obsolescence;

technology threatening constitutional objectives;

major modernization initiatives;

catastrophic interoperability failure;

enterprise-wide technology replacement.
