# Executive Summary

The Directorate of Planning is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for
transforming Executive Decisions into executable
Mission Plans.

A decision determines what shall be done.

Planning determines how it shall be accomplished.

The Directorate decomposes Executive Decisions into
objectives, campaigns, operations, tasks,
dependencies, milestones, schedules, resource
requirements, contingency plans, and measurable
success criteria.

Planning creates the executable blueprint that
enables disciplined Mission execution.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the
Directorate of Planning.

# MISSION

## Mission

The Directorate of Planning exists to transform
Executive Decisions into coordinated, executable,
resource-aware Mission Plans.

Its Mission is to ensure that every Executive
Decision becomes a coherent plan capable of guiding
successful execution.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Directorate preserves:

Mission feasibility;

planning discipline;

operational coordination;

resource alignment;

execution readiness;

Mission success.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Directorate shall:

develop Mission Plans;

coordinate campaign plans;

define operational objectives;

identify dependencies;

sequence activities;

evaluate feasibility;

prepare contingency plans;

continually improve planning capability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Decision establishes intent.

Planning establishes execution.

Execution accomplishes Mission.
