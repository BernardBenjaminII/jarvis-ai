# Executive Summary

The Directorate of Intelligence is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for
transforming Executive Knowledge into actionable
Executive Intelligence.

Knowledge describes what is known.

Intelligence determines what matters.

The Directorate continuously evaluates the
operational environment, identifies opportunities,
detects emerging risks, estimates future
developments, and produces intelligence products
that enable superior Executive Reasoning and
Decision-making.

The Directorate exists to reduce uncertainty before
Executive action is taken.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the
Directorate of Intelligence.

## Mission

The Directorate of Intelligence exists to produce
accurate, timely, relevant, and actionable
Executive Intelligence.

Its Mission is to transform institutional knowledge
into operational understanding that supports
Executive Reasoning, Executive Decision-making, and
Mission success.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Directorate preserves:

Executive awareness;

operational understanding;

strategic foresight;

risk awareness;

opportunity awareness;

decision superiority.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Directorate shall:

analyze Executive knowledge;

identify opportunities;

detect threats;

estimate future developments;

produce intelligence products;

maintain situational understanding;

support Executive decisions;

continually improve intelligence capability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Knowledge explains the present.

Intelligence estimates the future.

Future understanding strengthens Executive action.

Directorate.

# Executive Escalation

The following matters shall immediately escalate to
the Office of the Executive Director:

critical threat detection;

Mission-threatening intelligence;

high-confidence strategic opportunity;

systemic intelligence failure;

loss of intelligence capability.
