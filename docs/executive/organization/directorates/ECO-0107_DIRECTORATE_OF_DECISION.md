# Executive Summary

The Directorate of Decision is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for
transforming Executive Reasoning into authoritative
Executive Decisions.

Reasoning develops justified recommendations.

Decision commits the Executive Operating System to a
course of action.

The Directorate evaluates alternatives, balances
risk and opportunity, resolves uncertainty,
documents decision rationale, authorizes Executive
choices, and records every constitutional decision
for future accountability.

The Directorate ensures that Executive action is
deliberate, transparent, traceable, repeatable, and
constitutionally justified.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the
Directorate of Decision.

## Mission

The Directorate of Decision exists to convert
Executive understanding into authoritative
Executive Decisions.

Its Mission is to ensure that every Executive
Decision is justified, documented, accountable,
traceable, and aligned with constitutional purpose
and Mission success.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Directorate preserves:

Executive judgment;

decision accountability;

organizational commitment;

constitutional authority;

Mission alignment;

institutional responsibility.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Directorate shall:

evaluate recommendations;

approve Executive decisions;

balance opportunity and risk;

record decision rationale;

maintain decision history;

support Mission execution;

measure decision effectiveness;

continually improve decision quality.

---

# Constitutional Law

Reasoning explains alternatives.

Decision selects one.

Commitment transforms intention into action.
