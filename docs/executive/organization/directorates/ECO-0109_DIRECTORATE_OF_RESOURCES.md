# Executive Summary

The Directorate of Resources is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for
governing every resource required by the Executive
Operating System.

Planning determines what is required.

Resources determine what is available.

The Directorate manages personnel, knowledge,
compute, storage, finances, infrastructure,
equipment, time, energy, communications, and every
other constitutional asset required to accomplish
Executive Missions.

Its purpose is to ensure that every approved Mission
possesses the resources necessary for successful
execution while preserving efficiency,
accountability, sustainability, and long-term
organizational capability.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the
Directorate of Resources.

## Mission

The Directorate of Resources exists to acquire,
allocate, preserve, optimize, and govern the
resources of the Executive Operating System.

Its Mission is to ensure that every constitutional
organization possesses the capabilities required to
accomplish its assigned Mission while preserving
organizational sustainability.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Directorate preserves:

resource availability;

Mission readiness;

organizational sustainability;

resource accountability;

enterprise efficiency;

long-term capability.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Directorate shall:

allocate resources;

manage enterprise assets;

maintain resource inventories;

forecast resource requirements;

optimize utilization;

support Mission execution;

measure resource performance;

continually improve resource capability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Planning defines requirements.

Resources enable execution.

Well-governed resources sustain civilization.

Only the Directorate of Resources may establish
constitutional standards governing:

Directorate of Execution.
