# Executive Summary

The Directorate of Audit is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for
independently verifying the constitutional
integrity, operational compliance, accountability,
and institutional trustworthiness of the Executive
Operating System.

Assessment measures effectiveness.

Audit verifies legitimacy.

The Directorate independently examines Executive
organizations, constitutional processes, Mission
execution, evidence chains, decisions, resource
usage, certifications, and institutional behavior
to ensure that every Executive activity complies
with constitutional standards.

The Directorate exists to preserve trust.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the
Directorate of Audit.

## Mission

The Directorate of Audit exists to independently
verify constitutional compliance throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Its Mission is to preserve institutional trust by
objectively evaluating Executive activities,
identifying constitutional deviations, validating
accountability, and recommending corrective action.

The Directorate preserves:

constitutional integrity;

institutional accountability;

organizational trust;

Executive transparency;

independent verification;

continuous constitutional compliance.

# Primary Objectives

The Directorate shall:

conduct constitutional audits;

verify compliance;

evaluate governance;

audit Mission execution;

review Executive decisions;
