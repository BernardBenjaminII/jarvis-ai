# Executive Summary

The Directorate of Evidence is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for
transforming observations into validated Executive
Evidence.

Observation alone is insufficient for Executive
Decision-making.

Observations must be verified, authenticated,
classified, preserved, and assigned confidence
before becoming constitutional Evidence.

The Directorate of Evidence serves as the objective
truth authority of the Executive Operating System.

Its constitutional responsibility is to ensure that
every downstream capability—Learning, Memory,
Knowledge, Intelligence, Reasoning, Decision,
Planning, Execution, Assessment, and Audit—operates
only upon validated Evidence.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the
Directorate of Evidence.

## Mission

The Directorate of Evidence exists to establish
objective constitutional truth.

Its Mission is to transform observations into
validated, authenticated, traceable, measurable,
and constitutionally reliable Executive Evidence.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Directorate preserves:

objective truth;

Evidence integrity;

Evidence authenticity;

Executive confidence;

institutional trust;

constitutional legitimacy.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Directorate shall:

validate observations;

authenticate Evidence;

measure confidence;

classify Evidence;

preserve provenance;

maintain Evidence repositories;

support Executive cognition;

continually improve Evidence quality.

---

# Constitutional Law

Observation detects reality.

Evidence verifies reality.

Verified reality enables trustworthy Executive
reasoning.
