# Executive Summary

The Office of Constitutional Governance is the
constitutional guardian of the Executive Operating
System.

It exists to preserve, interpret, maintain,
certify, publish, and evolve the Constitution while
ensuring that every constitutional organization,
methodology, directive, policy, and Executive
activity remains consistent with constitutional
authority.

The Office neither executes Missions nor performs
operational work.

Instead, it preserves the legitimacy of the entire
Executive Government.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Office of
Constitutional Governance.
