# Executive Summary

The Office of Enterprise Coordination serves as the
constitutional institution responsible for
synchronizing every constitutional organization of
the Executive Operating System.

Where Strategic Planning determines where the
enterprise is going, and the Executive Director
determines what shall be accomplished, Enterprise
Coordination ensures that every constitutional
organization acts together as one integrated
Executive enterprise.

The Office continuously coordinates priorities,
resources, schedules, interdependencies,
communications, operational synchronization, and
organizational cooperation across every Executive
Directorate, Office, Board, Council, Authority,
Registry, and Repository.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Office of
Enterprise Coordination.

## Mission

The Office of Enterprise Coordination exists to
maintain operational unity throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Its Mission is to synchronize every constitutional
organization so that the Executive Government
functions as one coordinated enterprise rather than
independent organizations.

# Primary Objectives

The Office shall:

coordinate enterprise activities;

synchronize Executive organizations;

manage organizational dependencies;
