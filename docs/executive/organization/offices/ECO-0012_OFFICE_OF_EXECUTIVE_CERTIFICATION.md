# Executive Summary

The Office of Executive Certification is the
constitutional institution responsible for the
formal validation, certification, accreditation,
and constitutional approval of every permanent
Executive artifact produced throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Certification is the constitutional mechanism by
which Executive Authority declares that an artifact
is complete, compliant, authoritative, and suitable
for operational use.

The Office preserves trust by ensuring that nothing
becomes constitutionally authoritative without
independent certification.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Office of
Executive Certification.
