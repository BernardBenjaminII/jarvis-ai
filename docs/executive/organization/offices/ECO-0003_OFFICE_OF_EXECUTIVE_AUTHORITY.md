# Executive Summary

The Office of Executive Authority is the supreme
constitutional institution of the Executive
Operating System.

It exists to preserve the Constitution, govern the
Executive enterprise, establish constitutional
organizations, certify institutional capability,
approve constitutional change, safeguard Mission
purpose, and ensure the long-term continuity of the
Executive Operating System.

No constitutional organization possesses authority
equal to or greater than the Office of Executive
Authority.

Every constitutional organization ultimately serves
under its authority.

Accordingly, this document establishes the permanent
constitutional specification governing the Office of
Executive Authority.

## Mission

The Office of Executive Authority exists to govern
the Executive Operating System in accordance with
the Executive Constitution.

organization, methodology, Mission, decision,
resource, and capability remains constitutionally
legitimate while continually advancing the long-term
Mission of the Executive enterprise.
