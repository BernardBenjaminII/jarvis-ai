# Executive Summary

The Executive Asset Directorate is hereby
established as the permanent constitutional
organization responsible for governing the entire
enterprise portfolio of Executive Assets.

This Charter defines the Directorate's purpose,
authority, organizational scope,
responsibilities, relationships, and enduring
constitutional mandate.

The Directorate serves as the institutional
steward of every Executive Asset throughout its
complete lifecycle.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Directorate Charter.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Directorate Charter formally
establishes the constitutional authority,
responsibilities, and enduring purpose of the
Executive Asset Directorate.

Its objectives are:

constitutional stewardship;

enterprise governance;

institutional accountability;

asset excellence;

Executive support;

mission success.

---

# Constitutional Law

The Executive Asset Directorate shall remain the
authoritative constitutional steward of all
Executive Assets.

No Executive Asset shall exist outside its
governance.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# MISSION
# ==============================================================================

The mission of the Executive Asset Directorate is
to govern, protect, develop, coordinate,
modernize, and preserve every Executive Asset in
support of the Executive Constitution and the
constitutional mission of the Executive Operating
System.

---

# Directorate Vision

The Directorate shall build and preserve the most
capable, resilient, intelligent, and
constitutionally governed Executive Asset
enterprise possible.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART III
# AUTHORITY
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Directorate possesses
constitutional authority to:

register assets;

govern asset lifecycles;

approve certifications;

maintain asset registries;

coordinate Digital Twins;

supervise asset readiness;

maintain institutional memory;

issue Executive directives within delegated
authority.

Ultimate authority remains vested in the Executive
Constitution.
