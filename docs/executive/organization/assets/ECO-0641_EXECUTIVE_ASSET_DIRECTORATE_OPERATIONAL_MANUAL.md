# Executive Summary

The Executive Asset Directorate is the permanent
constitutional organization responsible for the
governance, stewardship, operation, and evolution
of every Executive Asset.

This Operational Manual defines how the
Directorate performs its constitutional duties,
coordinates subordinate organizations, executes
Executive authority, and maintains the enduring
health of the Executive Operating System.

Where constitutional standards define governing
law, this Manual defines constitutional practice.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Directorate Operational Manual.
