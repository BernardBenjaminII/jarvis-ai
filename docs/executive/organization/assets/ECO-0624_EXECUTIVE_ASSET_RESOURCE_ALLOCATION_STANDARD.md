# Executive Summary

Executive Assets exist to accomplish missions.

Resources exist to enable those assets.

Executive Resource Allocation is the constitutional
process through which personnel, funding,
infrastructure, computing capacity, knowledge,
time, materials, energy, and organizational
attention are assigned to maximize mission
success.

Resource allocation shall optimize the Executive
Operating System as an integrated enterprise.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Resource Allocation Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Resource Allocation Standard
establishes the constitutional framework for
allocating resources throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Its objectives are:

mission effectiveness;

resource stewardship;

Executive prioritization;

enterprise efficiency;

organizational resilience;

strategic flexibility.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional resource shall possess an
authorized allocation.

Resources shall support constitutional priorities.
