# Executive Summary

Every Executive Asset progresses through a
constitutional lifecycle.

The lifecycle governs the creation, development,
verification, certification, operational use,
maintenance, modernization, archival, retirement,
and historical preservation of every Executive
Asset.

No asset shall bypass lifecycle governance.

Lifecycle discipline preserves accountability,
traceability, operational readiness, and
institutional memory.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
constitutional lifecycle governing every Executive
Asset.

# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Lifecycle exists to ensure that
every asset progresses through a controlled,
observable, and auditable evolution.

Its objectives are:

predictability;

traceability;

quality;

operational readiness;

historical continuity;

constitutional accountability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every asset has a beginning.

Every asset evolves.

Every asset leaves a permanent history.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Every Executive Asset shall:

begin in a registered state;

advance only through authorized transitions;

maintain historical continuity;

preserve certification history;

retain complete operational records;

remain permanently traceable.

Lifecycle history shall never be deleted.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Lifecycle progression shall be:

observable;

authorized;

repeatable;

measurable;

auditable;

historically preserved.

Registered

Identity established.

Planned

Executive approval to proceed.

Designed

Architecture completed.

Implemented

Asset constructed.

Integrated

Connected to enterprise.

Verified

Requirements confirmed.

Validated

Operational suitability confirmed.

Certified

Executive authorization granted.

Operational

Active service.

Monitored

Operational performance supervised.

Maintained

Modernized

Capability improved.

Superseded

Retired
