# Executive Summary

Executive authority is inseparable from Executive
oversight.

Every constitutional asset, regardless of its
autonomy, complexity, capability, or operational
role, shall remain continuously subject to
constitutional supervision.

Constitutional Oversight provides the Executive
Operating System with the authority, visibility,
controls, and accountability necessary to ensure
that all Executive Assets remain aligned with the
Executive Constitution.

Oversight preserves constitutional legitimacy.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Constitutional Oversight Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Constitutional Oversight
Standard establishes the constitutional framework
governing oversight throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Its objectives are:

constitutional governance;

Executive visibility;

continuous accountability;

mission assurance;

institutional discipline;

organizational integrity.

---

# Constitutional Law

No constitutional asset shall operate without
continuous Executive oversight.

Oversight authority shall never be delegated
beyond constitutional limits.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Executive Oversight shall be:

continuous;

objective;

evidence-based;

auditable;

traceable;

constitutionally governed;

historically preserved.

operations.
