# Executive Summary

The Executive Asset Directorate shall be organized
to provide clear constitutional authority,
enterprise-wide coordination, operational
discipline, and continuous stewardship of all
Executive Assets.

Organizational structure shall maximize
accountability while minimizing ambiguity.

Every constitutional responsibility shall possess
a clearly identified owner.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Directorate Organizational
Structure.

# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Directorate Organizational
Structure establishes the constitutional
organization responsible for governing Executive
Assets.

Its objectives are:

clear authority;

organizational accountability;

operational efficiency;

mission coordination;

constitutional governance;

institutional continuity.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional responsibility shall possess
an assigned organizational owner.

Authority and responsibility shall remain aligned.
