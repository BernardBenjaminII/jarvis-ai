# Executive Summary

No Executive Asset exists in isolation.

Every constitutional asset forms part of a larger
Executive ecosystem composed of organizations,
missions, capabilities, infrastructure,
knowledge, software, hardware, personnel,
Executive authorities, and external systems.

Understanding these relationships is essential for
Executive reasoning, operational planning,
dependency analysis, impact assessment,
institutional learning, and constitutional
governance.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Relationship Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Relationship Standard defines
the constitutional model governing relationships
between Executive Assets.

Its objectives are:

organizational visibility;

dependency management;

impact analysis;

Executive reasoning;

institutional understanding;

enterprise coordination.

---

# Constitutional Law

Executive Assets shall never exist without
constitutional context.

Context is defined through relationships.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Every relationship shall be:

explicit;

traceable;

versioned;

auditable;

directional;

machine-readable;

constitutionally authorized.

Relationships shall never be inferred without
supporting evidence.

# Relationship Principles

Relationships preserve:

organizational structure;

operational dependencies;

mission alignment;

knowledge continuity;

Executive accountability.
