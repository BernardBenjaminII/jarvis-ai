# Executive Summary

Every Executive Asset shall be governed from its
constitutional conception through its permanent
historical preservation.

Lifecycle Governance provides continuous
Executive oversight across acquisition,
registration, operation, modernization,
certification, retirement, and archival while
preserving constitutional identity and
institutional accountability.

Lifecycle Governance ensures that no Executive
Asset exists outside constitutional authority at
any stage of its existence.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Lifecycle Governance Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Lifecycle Governance Standard
establishes the constitutional framework
governing Executive Assets throughout their
entire lifecycle.

Its objectives are:

continuous governance;

institutional accountability;

mission continuity;

constitutional compliance;

historical preservation;

Executive stewardship.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall remain under
Executive governance throughout its entire
lifecycle.

No lifecycle stage shall exist without an
identified governing authority.
