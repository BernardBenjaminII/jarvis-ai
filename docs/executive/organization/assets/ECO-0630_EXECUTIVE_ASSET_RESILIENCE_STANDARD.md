# Executive Summary

Failures are inevitable.

Mission failure is not.

Executive Asset Resilience is the constitutional
ability of every Executive Asset to anticipate,
withstand, adapt to, recover from, and learn from
adverse conditions while continuing to support
constitutional missions.

Resilience transforms disruption into adaptation.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Resilience Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Resilience Standard
establishes the constitutional framework
governing resilience throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Its objectives are:

mission endurance;

organizational adaptability;

Executive confidence;

operational robustness;

continuous learning;

institutional survival.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall possess an
approved resilience strategy.

Mission-critical assets shall demonstrate
resilience through evidence.

Asset Resilience Standard as the permanent

uncertainty, or adversity.
