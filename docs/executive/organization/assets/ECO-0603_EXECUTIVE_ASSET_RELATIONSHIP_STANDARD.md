# Executive Summary

No Executive Asset exists in isolation.

Every Executive Asset participates in a network of
constitutional relationships that collectively
describe the structure, behavior, dependencies,
authority, and operational interactions of the
Executive Operating System.

The Executive Asset Relationship Standard
establishes the constitutional model governing
asset relationships, dependency graphs, ownership,
communication paths, operational influence, and
historical traceability.

The complete enterprise shall therefore exist as a
constitutional knowledge graph.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Relationship Standard.
