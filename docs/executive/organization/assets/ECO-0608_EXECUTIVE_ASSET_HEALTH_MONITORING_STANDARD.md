# Executive Summary

The Executive Operating System shall maintain
continuous awareness of the health and operational
condition of every constitutional asset.

Asset health extends beyond availability.

It includes operational readiness, performance,
security posture, reliability, sustainability,
maintenance status, mission fitness, and predicted
future condition.

Health monitoring provides the Executive with the
situational awareness necessary for timely
decision-making and institutional resilience.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Health Monitoring Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Health Monitoring Standard
establishes the constitutional framework governing
continuous health assessment of Executive Assets.

Its objectives are:

continuous visibility;

operational readiness;

predictive maintenance;

Executive awareness;

institutional resilience;

risk reduction.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall possess an
observable health status.

Unknown health is unacceptable.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Health monitoring shall be:

continuous;

objective;

evidence-based;

automated where practical;

auditable;

historically preserved;

constitutionally governed.

Health assessments shall rely upon measurable
evidence.

# Monitoring Principles

Monitoring shall preserve:

operational confidence;

mission readiness;

organizational transparency;

historical trends;

Executive accountability.
