# Executive Summary

Every Executive Asset shall possess measurable
performance objectives throughout its
constitutional lifecycle.

Performance measurement provides the Executive
Operating System with objective evidence that
assets are fulfilling their constitutional
purpose.

Performance shall be continuously evaluated,
historically preserved, and incorporated into
Executive planning, modernization, certification,
and mission readiness.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Performance Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Performance Standard
establishes the constitutional framework for
measuring and governing the performance of
Executive Assets.

Its objectives are:

objective measurement;

continuous improvement;

Executive accountability;

mission optimization;

resource efficiency;

institutional excellence.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall possess
measurable performance objectives.

Performance shall be measured through evidence,
never opinion.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Performance evaluation shall be:

objective;

repeatable;

auditable;

evidence-based;

historically preserved;

constitutionally governed;

machine-readable.

Performance measurements shall never be altered
after publication.

Corrections shall be recorded as new Executive
Events.
