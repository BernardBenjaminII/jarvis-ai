# Executive Summary

The Executive Operating System shall continuously
increase its constitutional maturity.

Organizational excellence is not a fixed state.

It is a measurable progression.

The Executive Asset Directorate Maturity Model
establishes the constitutional framework for
evaluating the maturity of Executive governance,
operations, knowledge, technology, automation,
organizational capability, and institutional
effectiveness.

Every assessment shall identify the current state,
the desired future state, and the constitutional
path required to achieve it.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Directorate Maturity Model.

# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Directorate Maturity Model
establishes the constitutional framework
governing institutional maturity throughout the
Executive Asset Directorate.

Its objectives are:

institutional excellence;

continuous advancement;

Executive visibility;

organizational development;

mission effectiveness;

constitutional evolution.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional organization shall possess
an identified maturity level.

Institutional maturity shall improve over time.
