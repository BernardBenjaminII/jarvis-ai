# Executive Summary

Every Executive Asset shall evolve throughout its
constitutional lifetime.

Modernization preserves institutional relevance,
operational superiority, security, efficiency,
and mission effectiveness while maintaining
constitutional continuity.

Modernization is a planned constitutional process,
not an ad hoc replacement of assets.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Modernization Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Modernization Standard
establishes the constitutional framework for
planning, approving, executing, and governing the
modernization of Executive Assets.

Its objectives are:

institutional evolution;

mission superiority;

technology refresh;

continuous improvement;

Executive accountability;

long-term sustainability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall possess a
modernization strategy.

Assets shall evolve before they become obsolete.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Modernization shall be:

planned;

incremental where practical;

evidence-based;

auditable;

constitutionally approved;

mission-driven;

historically preserved.

Modernization shall preserve constitutional
identity unless explicitly superseded by
Executive authority.

---

# Modernization Principles

Modernization preserves:

mission capability;

institutional investment;

organizational continuity;

knowledge retention;

Executive confidence.
