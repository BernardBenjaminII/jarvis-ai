# Executive Summary

Possessing capability is insufficient.

Capability must be immediately available for
constitutional employment.

Executive Readiness measures the ability of an
Executive Asset to perform its assigned mission at
the required level of effectiveness, within the
required timeframe, and under expected operating
conditions.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Readiness Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Readiness Standard establishes
the constitutional framework governing readiness
throughout the Executive Operating System.

Its objectives are:

mission preparedness;

operational confidence;

Executive awareness;

resource optimization;

institutional resilience;

continuous readiness.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall possess a current
readiness assessment.

Unknown readiness constitutes Executive risk.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Executive Readiness shall be:

objective;

measurable;

continuously updated;

evidence-based;

# ==============================================================================
# PART VI
# MISSION READINESS
# ==============================================================================

Mission readiness determines whether an Executive
Asset can immediately support an assigned mission.

Mission readiness shall consider:

required capabilities;

resource availability;

operational constraints;

mission dependencies;

environmental conditions;

expected duration.

Mission readiness shall support Executive planning
and mission allocation.
