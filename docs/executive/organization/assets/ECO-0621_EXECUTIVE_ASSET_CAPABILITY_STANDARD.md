# Executive Summary

Executive Assets exist for one constitutional
purpose:

to provide capability.

Capability is the measurable ability of an
Executive Asset to perform one or more
constitutional functions in support of Executive
missions.

The Executive Operating System shall understand,
measure, certify, allocate, and continuously
improve every constitutional capability.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Capability Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Capability Standard
establishes the constitutional framework
governing Executive capabilities throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Its objectives are:

capability visibility;

mission readiness;

resource optimization;

Executive planning;

institutional growth;

continuous capability improvement.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall declare its
capabilities.

Unknown capability constitutes Executive
uncertainty.
