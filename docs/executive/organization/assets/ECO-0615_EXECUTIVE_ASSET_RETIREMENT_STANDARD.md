# Executive Summary

Every Executive Asset shall eventually reach the
end of its constitutional operational lifetime.

Retirement is a controlled constitutional process
that preserves institutional knowledge,
operational continuity, historical accountability,
and Executive governance while safely removing an
asset from active service.

Retirement shall never result in the loss of
institutional memory.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Retirement Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Retirement Standard
establishes the constitutional framework for
retiring Executive Assets throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Its objectives are:

controlled decommissioning;

knowledge preservation;

risk reduction;

historical continuity;

Executive accountability;

institutional stability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall possess a
retirement strategy before entering operational
service.

Retirement shall preserve institutional history.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Retirement shall be:

planned;

authorized;

auditable;

evidence-based;

historically preserved;

constitutionally governed;

irreversible after certification.

No constitutional history shall be destroyed
during retirement.

---

# Retirement Principles

Retirement preserves:

institutional memory;

organizational continuity;

Executive accountability;

historical integrity;

mission stability.

# ==============================================================================
# PART VI
# KNOWLEDGE PRESERVATION
# ==============================================================================

Before retirement, every Executive Asset shall
transfer all constitutional knowledge to the
institution.

Knowledge preservation shall include:

configuration history;

Executive Events;

maintenance history;

performance history;

relationships;

Digital Twin;

certifications;

historical records.

Institutional knowledge shall survive the asset.
