# Executive Summary

Executive Assets exist to accomplish
constitutional missions.

The Executive Asset Directorate exists to ensure
that this capability continuously improves.

The Performance Framework establishes the
constitutional methodology for measuring,
evaluating, reporting, and improving the
effectiveness of Executive Asset governance,
operations, readiness, stewardship, and mission
support.

Performance shall be measurable.

Improvement shall be continuous.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Directorate Performance
Framework.

# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Directorate Performance
Framework establishes the constitutional
framework governing performance management
throughout the Executive Asset Directorate.

Its objectives are:

objective measurement;

Executive accountability;

mission effectiveness;

organizational excellence;

continuous improvement;

institutional maturity.

---

# Constitutional Law

Executive performance shall be measured using
objective constitutional evidence.

Performance shall improve continuously.
