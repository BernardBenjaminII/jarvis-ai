# Executive Summary

Executive leadership requires more than data.

It requires understanding.

Executive Analytics transforms constitutional
information into actionable Executive insight
through measurement, correlation, trend analysis,
forecasting, visualization, and institutional
learning.

Analytics enables the Executive Operating System
to understand what happened, why it happened,
what is happening now, what is likely to happen
next, and what actions should be considered.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Analytics Standard.

# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Analytics Standard establishes
the constitutional framework governing analytical
activities throughout the Executive Operating
System.

Its objectives are:

Executive understanding;

enterprise visibility;

predictive capability;

institutional optimization;

strategic awareness;

continuous improvement.

# Constitutional Law

Executive decisions shall be informed through
constitutional analytics whenever evidence is
available.
