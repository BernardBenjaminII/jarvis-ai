# Executive Summary

The Executive Operating System is a unified
constitutional enterprise composed of numerous
Executive Assets.

No asset shall function as an isolated system.

Interoperability enables Executive Assets to
communicate, cooperate, exchange information,
coordinate operations, and accomplish missions as
a single constitutional organization.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Interoperability Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Interoperability Standard
establishes the constitutional framework
governing interoperability throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Its objectives are:

enterprise integration;

mission coordination;

organizational cohesion;

technology compatibility;

information exchange;

Executive unity.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall interoperate
through approved Executive interfaces.

Isolated systems are prohibited.
