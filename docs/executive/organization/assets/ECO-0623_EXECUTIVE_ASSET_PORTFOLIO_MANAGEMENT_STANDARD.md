# Executive Summary

Executive Assets shall not be governed solely as
individual resources.

They shall also be managed collectively as an
integrated constitutional portfolio whose purpose
is to maximize mission effectiveness, institutional
value, strategic flexibility, and Executive
readiness.

Portfolio Management enables Executive leadership
to understand the composition, balance, health,
risk, capability, cost, and evolution of the
enterprise.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Portfolio Management Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Portfolio Management Standard
establishes the constitutional framework governing
the enterprise portfolio of Executive Assets.

Its objectives are:

enterprise optimization;

strategic alignment;

resource stewardship;

mission effectiveness;

Executive visibility;

institutional sustainability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every Executive Asset shall belong to one or more
constitutional portfolios.

No asset shall exist outside portfolio governance.

---
