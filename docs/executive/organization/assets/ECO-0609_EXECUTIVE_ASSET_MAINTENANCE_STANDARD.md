# Executive Summary

Every Executive Asset shall remain capable of
performing its constitutional responsibilities
throughout its operational lifetime.

Maintenance is a constitutional function rather
than a corrective activity.

Its purpose is to preserve operational readiness,
maximize service life, reduce institutional risk,
and ensure continuous mission capability.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Maintenance Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Maintenance Standard
establishes the constitutional framework
governing maintenance activities throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Its objectives are:

operational readiness;

maximum asset longevity;

institutional reliability;

mission continuity;

Executive accountability;

predictable performance.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall possess an
approved maintenance program.

Deferred maintenance shall constitute
constitutional risk.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Maintenance shall be:

planned;

traceable;

evidence-based;

auditable;

risk-informed;

historically preserved;

constitutionally authorized.

Every maintenance activity shall generate an
Executive Event.

# ==============================================================================

# ==============================================================================
# PART VI
# MAINTENANCE RECORDS
# ==============================================================================

Every maintenance activity shall produce a
