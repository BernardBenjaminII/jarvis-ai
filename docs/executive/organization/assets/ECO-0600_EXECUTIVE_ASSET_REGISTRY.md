# Executive Summary

The Executive Asset Registry (EAR) is the
permanent constitutional registry responsible for
establishing, identifying, governing, tracking,
and preserving every asset belonging to the
Executive Operating System.

An Executive Operating System cannot govern
objects that possess no identity.

Accordingly, every physical, digital,
organizational, informational, computational,
logical, and autonomous capability shall exist as
a constitutional Executive Asset.

The Registry serves as the authoritative source of
truth describing the identity, ownership,
relationships, lifecycle, certification status,
operational history, and accountability of every
Executive Asset.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
constitutional specification governing the
Executive Asset Registry.
