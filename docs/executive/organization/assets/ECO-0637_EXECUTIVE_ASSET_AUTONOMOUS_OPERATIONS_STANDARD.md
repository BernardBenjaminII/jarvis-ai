# Executive Summary

Executive Assets shall increasingly execute
constitutional responsibilities with appropriate
levels of autonomy.

Autonomy shall increase operational speed,
organizational efficiency, mission responsiveness,
and institutional scalability while preserving
Executive authority, constitutional oversight,
auditability, and accountability.

Autonomous execution shall never replace
constitutional governance.

Executive authority shall always remain supreme.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Autonomous Operations Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Autonomous Operations
Standard establishes the constitutional framework
governing autonomous execution throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Its objectives are:

operational efficiency;

mission responsiveness;

institutional scalability;

Executive oversight;

constitutional accountability;

controlled autonomy.

---

# Constitutional Law

Executive Assets may execute autonomously only
within constitutionally authorized authority.

Executive authority shall remain the final
decision authority.

# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES

AUTO-<CATEGORY>-<NUMBER>

Examples

reused.

# ==============================================================================
# PART IV
# AUTONOMY LEVELS
# ==============================================================================

Executive Assets shall operate under one of the
following constitutional autonomy levels.

Level 0

Direct Executive Control

Level 1

Executive Assisted Operations

Level 2

Supervised Autonomous Operations

Level 3

Delegated Autonomous Execution

Level 4

Mission Autonomous Operations

Level 5

Executive Constitutional Autonomy

Each level shall define:

authorized decisions;

approval requirements;

supervision requirements;

escalation criteria.
