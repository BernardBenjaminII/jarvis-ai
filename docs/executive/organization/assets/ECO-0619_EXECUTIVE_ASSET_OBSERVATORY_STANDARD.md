# Executive Summary

The Executive Operating System shall possess
continuous constitutional awareness of every
Executive Asset.

The Executive Observatory is the enterprise-wide
capability responsible for observing, collecting,
correlating, visualizing, and preserving the
operational state of the Executive Operating
System.

Its purpose is not merely monitoring.

Its purpose is Executive situational awareness.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Observatory Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Observatory Standard
establishes the constitutional framework for
enterprise observability throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Its objectives are:

continuous awareness;

enterprise visibility;

Executive oversight;

operational transparency;

historical preservation;

strategic readiness.

---

# Constitutional Law

No constitutional asset shall exist outside the
Executive Observatory.

Assets that cannot be observed cannot be governed.
