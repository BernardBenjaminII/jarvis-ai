# Executive Summary

Executive Assets are institutional resources that
exist solely to accomplish constitutional
missions.

Governance establishes the authority,
responsibilities, accountability, oversight,
decision-making processes, and stewardship
required to ensure every Executive Asset serves
the interests of the Executive Operating System.

Governance transforms a collection of assets into
a coordinated constitutional enterprise.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Governance Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Governance Standard
establishes the constitutional framework for
governing Executive Assets throughout the
Executive Operating System.

Its objectives are:

constitutional oversight;

Executive accountability;

institutional stewardship;

mission alignment;

organizational transparency;

long-term sustainability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every Executive Asset shall possess an identified
governing authority.

Assets without governance shall not operate.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

constitutional;

transparent;

accountable;

auditable;

evidence-based;

mission-driven;

historically preserved.

assigned.
