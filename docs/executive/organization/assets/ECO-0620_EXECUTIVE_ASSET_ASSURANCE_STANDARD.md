# Executive Summary

Executive leadership shall possess justified
confidence that every constitutional asset
operates exactly as intended.

Executive Assurance is the institutional process
through which evidence from governance,
compliance, auditing, performance, security,
health, risk, certification, and observation is
integrated into a single constitutional judgment
regarding trustworthiness.

Assurance is confidence justified by evidence.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Assurance Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Assurance Standard establishes
the constitutional framework governing assurance
throughout the Executive Operating System.

Its objectives are:

Executive confidence;

institutional trust;

continuous verification;

mission assurance;

organizational resilience;

constitutional integrity.

---

# Constitutional Law

Executive authority shall rely upon evidence-based
assurance rather than assumption.

Unverified confidence is prohibited.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Executive Assurance shall be:

evidence-based;

objective;

repeatable;

auditable;

transparent;

historically preserved;

constitutionally governed.
