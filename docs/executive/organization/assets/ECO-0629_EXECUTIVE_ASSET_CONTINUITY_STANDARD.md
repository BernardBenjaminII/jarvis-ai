# Executive Summary

The Executive Operating System shall continue to
fulfill its constitutional mission despite
failures, disruptions, disasters, or unexpected
events.

Executive Asset Continuity ensures that critical
assets remain available, recoverable,
replaceable, and capable of supporting
constitutional operations under all reasonably
anticipated conditions.

Continuity preserves institutional existence.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Continuity Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Continuity Standard
establishes the constitutional framework
governing continuity throughout the Executive
Operating System.

Its objectives are:

operational resilience;

mission continuity;

rapid recovery;

institutional preservation;

Executive preparedness;

organizational endurance.

---

# Constitutional Law

Every constitutional asset shall possess an
approved continuity strategy.

Mission-critical assets shall possess tested
recovery procedures.
