# Executive Summary

Identity is the constitutional foundation of every
Executive Asset.

No Executive capability may exist without a unique,
immutable, and traceable identity.

The Executive Asset Identity Standard defines the
canonical structure, metadata, naming convention,
identifier format, ownership model, and lifecycle
requirements governing every Executive Asset.

The purpose of this Standard is to ensure that
every asset can be uniquely identified, located,
governed, audited, certified, and historically
traced throughout its complete constitutional
lifecycle.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Identity Standard.

# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

This Standard exists to establish one immutable
constitutional identity for every Executive Asset.

Its objectives are to guarantee:

uniqueness;

traceability;

discoverability;

interoperability;

historical continuity;

institutional accountability.

---

# Constitutional Law

Identity precedes ownership.

Ownership precedes responsibility.

Responsibility preserves accountability.

---

# ==============================================================================
# PART II
# IDENTITY PRINCIPLES
# ==============================================================================

Every Executive Asset shall possess:

one canonical identity;

one canonical owner;

one constitutional classification;

one lifecycle state;

one certification state;

one operational history.

Identity shall never be reused.

Retired identities remain permanently reserved.

---

# Identity Characteristics

Identity shall be:

globally unique;

immutable;

machine-readable;

human-readable;

version-aware;

searchable;

constitutionally governed.

# ==============================================================================
# PART III
# EXECUTIVE ASSET IDENTIFIER
# ==============================================================================

Every asset shall receive an Executive Asset
Identifier (EAID).

Canonical format:

EA-<CLASS>-<NUMBER>

Examples

EA-SW-000001

EA-AI-000001

EA-KN-000001

EA-RB-000001

EA-INF-000001

EA-DOC-000001

EA-ORG-000001

EA-MSN-000001

EA-DS-000001

EA-SVC-000001

The numeric identifier shall never be reused.
