# Executive Summary

Constitutional compliance shall not be assumed.

It shall be continuously demonstrated.

Executive Constitutional Compliance Assurance
integrates governance, compliance, oversight,
auditing, certification, monitoring, Executive
Events, and institutional evidence to provide
continuous assurance that every Executive Asset
operates in accordance with the Executive
Constitution.

Constitutional compliance shall be continuously
observable, measurable, and verifiable.

Accordingly, this document establishes the
Executive Asset Constitutional Compliance
Assurance Standard.

# ==============================================================================
# PART I
# PURPOSE
# ==============================================================================

The Executive Asset Constitutional Compliance
Assurance Standard establishes the constitutional
framework governing continuous compliance
assurance throughout the Executive Operating
System.

Its objectives are:

constitutional confidence;

continuous verification;

Executive accountability;

institutional integrity;

mission assurance;

organizational trust.

---

# Constitutional Law

Constitutional compliance shall be continuously
demonstrated through objective evidence.

Absence of evidence shall not constitute evidence
of compliance.
