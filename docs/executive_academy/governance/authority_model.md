# Authority Model

## Tier A — Supreme and Primary Authority

- Constitutions
- Treaties
- Statutes
- Codes
- Military codes

## Tier B — Binding Executive and Administrative Authority

- Regulations
- Executive orders
- Administrative rules
- Official gazettes
- Binding government directives

## Tier C — Adjudicative Authority

- Judicial opinions
- Administrative decisions
- Tribunal decisions

## Tier D — Official Interpretive and Operational Authority

- Government guidance
- Standards
- Military doctrine
- Official manuals
- Agency publications

## Tier E — Secondary Authority

- Academic literature
- Professional commentary
- Technical references
- Treatises

Authority classification does not alone establish truth. Applicability,
jurisdiction, temporal validity, provenance, and conflict analysis remain
required.
