# Executive Academy Master Plan

**Planning horizon:** 2026–2035  
**Baseline:** EAF-001

## Vision

Create an institutional intelligence system that can distinguish between
having information, understanding a field, and being qualified to advise or act.

## Strategic Outcomes

- Canonical departments and academies
- Global authority and jurisdiction registries
- Measurable knowledge coverage
- Structured curricula and prerequisite graphs
- Exercises and executive simulations
- Evidence-based competency assessment
- Scoped and revocable certification
- Transparent capability and knowledge-gap reporting
- Integration with Mission Control after Genesis certification

## Program Sequence

1. EAF-001 — Constitutional Foundation
2. EAF-002 — Department and Academy Registry
3. EAF-003 — Authority and Source Governance
4. EAF-004 — Global Jurisdiction Registry
5. EAF-005 — Curriculum Framework
6. EAF-006 — Competency Framework
7. EAF-007 — Simulation Framework
8. EAF-008 — Certification Framework
9. EAF-009 — Knowledge Acquisition Integration
10. EAF-010 — Executive Academy Activation

## Activation Gate

Operational implementation is prohibited until:

- Genesis integration boundaries are certified.
- Canonical knowledge ownership is stable.
- Academy schemas and public contracts are frozen.
- A runtime activation ADR is approved.
- Regression verification demonstrates no Genesis dependency inversion.
