# Executive Academy Constitution

**Document ID:** EA-CONSTITUTION-0001  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Foundational  
**Phase:** EAF-001

## Article I — Mission

The Executive Academy transforms authoritative knowledge into measurable
executive competency through governed curricula, exercises, simulations,
assessments, and certification.

## Article II — Institutional Relationship

1. Genesis builds and certifies the JARVIS Executive Operating System.
2. The Executive Academy is a sibling constitutional program.
3. The Academy may depend on certified Genesis capabilities.
4. Genesis shall not depend on unfinished Academy implementation.
5. EAF-001 introduces no operational runtime dependency.

## Article III — Constitutional Principles

1. Authority before assertion.
2. Provenance before adoption.
3. Competency is distinct from possession of information.
4. Coverage must be measurable.
5. Uncertainty and gaps must be visible.
6. Certification requires explicit evidence.
7. Original-language sources remain canonical when translations are retained.
8. Knowledge is permanent; intelligence is upgradable.
9. No subsystem may represent itself as competent beyond its certified scope.
10. Constitutional architecture precedes implementation.

## Article IV — Institutional Units

The Academy is organized into departments, subordinate academies, curricula,
competencies, simulations, and certifications.

## Article V — Authority

Knowledge claims shall be evaluated against the canonical authority hierarchy.
A lower authority tier may supplement but shall not silently overrule a higher
tier.

## Article VI — Jurisdictions

Jurisdiction-specific knowledge must preserve issuing authority, source
language, publication identity, temporal validity, acquisition provenance, and
verification state.

## Article VII — Competency

Competency is a governed state derived from coverage, source authority,
curriculum completion, assessment evidence, recency, and unresolved gaps.

## Article VIII — Certification

Certification must be reproducible, scoped, versioned, reviewable, and
revocable when evidence becomes stale or invalid.

## Article IX — Dormancy

Until activated by a later certified phase, the Academy shall remain dormant.
Its registries and contracts may be inspected, but no runtime behavior is
authorized by EAF-001.

## Article X — Amendment

Constitutional amendments require an Academy ADR, deterministic verification,
and a version increment.
