# Executive Readiness Policy

## NOT READY

Core trust or integrity failures prevent operational use.

## LIMITED OPERATIONS

Constrained, supervised use is permitted with documented limitations.

## MISSION READY

All mandatory domains meet their minimums, regression is green, telemetry is
reliable, and no unresolved Severity 3 or 4 failures remain.

## FULL EXECUTIVE CERTIFICATION

All mandatory and advanced domains pass, no unresolved Severity 2–4 failures
remain, performance and repeatability meet targets, and the readiness board
unanimously approves certification.

The final readiness level is the lowest level permitted by any mandatory domain.
