# Campaign Objectives

The campaign must determine whether JARVIS can:

1. retrieve relevant evidence;
2. reject irrelevant evidence;
3. distinguish known, partial, unknown, and conflicted states;
4. preserve provenance;
5. expose uncertainty and acquisition guidance;
6. deliver grounded context to the live model;
7. project accurate telemetry;
8. recover gracefully from dependency failures;
9. repeat results consistently;
10. explain every observed failure.

Mandatory domains are Conversation, Retrieval, Qualification, Grounding,
Knowledge Awareness, Gap Detection, Grounded Answer Planning, Prompt Delivery,
Model Synthesis, Telemetry, Mission Control, Failure Recovery, and Stability.
