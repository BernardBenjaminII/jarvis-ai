## Mission

Replace empty-success verification with deterministic demonstrations of all four operational compliance outcomes against the live C3 constitutional corpus.

## Scenarios

Pack 2 deterministically selects one ratified constitutional article with sufficient lexical material and derives:

1. **Positive compliance** — exact canonical text.
2. **Negative compliance** — canonical text with inverted normative polarity.
3. **Review required** — deterministic partial lexical representation.
4. **Not applicable** — unrelated content in an unrelated domain.

## Certification requirements

Pack 2 fails unless:

- four scenarios execute;
- all four pass;
- at least one compliant finding exists;
- at least one noncompliant finding exists;
- at least one review-required finding exists;
- at least one subject is not applicable;
- the expected article ranks first for each applicable scenario;
- all scenario results carry the complete C0–C4 fingerprint chain;
- repeated execution produces the same certification fingerprint.
