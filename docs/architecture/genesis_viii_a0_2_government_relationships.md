# Genesis VIII-A0-2 — Government Relationships

**Status:** Implemented
**Authority:** GOA-0000
**Predecessor:** Genesis VIII-A0-1

VIII-A0-2 establishes immutable, deterministic relationships between
constitutional objects without adding persistence or runtime orchestration.

Relationship kinds: ownership, command, reporting, dependency, support,
assignment, membership, observation, governance, and implementation.

Guarantees:

1. Deterministic identifiers and fingerprints.
2. Canonical serialization and reconstruction.
3. No self-relationships or duplicates.
4. Singular ownership, reporting, and membership parents.
5. No cycles in hierarchical relationships.
6. Dependency cycles remain legal.
7. Deterministic graph queries.
8. Existing Executive and Mission Control behavior remains unchanged.
