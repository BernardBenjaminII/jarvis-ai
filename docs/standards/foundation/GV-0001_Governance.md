# GV-0001 — Executive Governance

---

# 1. Purpose

This standard defines the governance model for the JARVIS Executive.

Governance ensures that the Executive evolves deliberately, transparently, and under authorized human oversight while preserving its constitutional identity.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard governs:

- constitutional authority
- architectural authority
- standards management
- architectural evolution
- approval processes
- delegation of authority
- governance responsibilities

It does not govern operational mission execution.

---

# 3. Authority

This standard derives its authority from FP-0000.

No governance process may contradict FP-0000.

---

# 4. Governance Principles

## GV-001 — Constitutional Supremacy

FP-0000 shall remain the supreme engineering authority for the Executive.

---

## GV-002 — Human Authority

Ultimate authority over the Executive remains with the Commander or delegated governing authority.

---

## GV-003 — No Autonomous Constitutional Change

The Executive shall not independently modify:

- First Principles
- Governance
- Character
- Safety

---

## GV-004 — Evidence-Based Evolution

Every proposed architectural change shall include:

- motivation
- supporting evidence
- expected benefits
- risks
- compatibility assessment
- verification strategy

---

## GV-005 — Traceable Decisions

Every constitutional decision shall be documented through an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

---

## GV-006 — Backward Integrity

Architectural evolution shall preserve constitutional integrity unless explicitly superseded through governance.

---

## GV-007 — Transparency

Governance decisions shall remain reviewable and auditable.

---

## GV-008 — Separation of Governance and Implementation

Implementation shall follow governance.

Governance shall not be dictated by implementation convenience.

---

# 5. Governance Responsibilities

Commander

- establishes vision
- approves constitutional change
- delegates authority

Architecture Board

- authors standards
- reviews proposals
- evaluates compatibility
- recommends approval

Executive

- complies with governance
- provides analysis
- recommends improvements
- does not approve constitutional changes
