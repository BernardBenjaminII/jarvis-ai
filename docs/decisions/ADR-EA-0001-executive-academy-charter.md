# ADR-EA-0001 — Executive Academy Charter

**Status:** Accepted  
**Phase:** EAF-001

## Context

JARVIS requires a permanent institutional framework for transforming
authoritative knowledge into measurable executive competency. This framework
must be preserved now without disrupting active Genesis implementation.

## Decision

Establish the Executive Academy as a sibling constitutional program to Genesis.

EAF-001 shall introduce documentation, registries, schemas, immutable
contracts, tests, and deterministic verification only. Operational runtime
behavior is explicitly prohibited.

## Consequences

- The idea becomes a certified repository asset rather than a backlog note.
- Genesis continues without dependency on unfinished Academy capabilities.
- Future Academy phases inherit canonical names, boundaries, and governance.
- Activation requires a later ADR and certified phase.
