# ADR-0038 — Canonical Observation Convergence
**Status:** Accepted  
**Milestone:** Genesis IV-B3  
**Standard:** OCS-0000

## Decision
Ratify `core.observation` as the constitutional owner of the canonical Observation contract. Existing cognition extraction and Executive Observation Bus implementations remain supported as legacy producers and transports. Explicit adapters translate their outputs. New producers emit the canonical contract directly.

The contract supports Live Reality and Recorded Reality. Books, files, PDFs, source code, images, transcripts, databases, and archives are first-class sources.

## Consequences
No destructive migration occurs. Certified phases remain testable. New duplicate definitions are prohibited. Evidence and later Executive phases receive one stable identity and provenance model.
